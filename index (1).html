Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does solar cost?
Cost depends entirely on your energy usage — a small home backup starts modestly, while a full hybrid system for a large household or business costs more. We run a free energy audit first so your quote reflects exactly what you need, not a generic package.
How long does installation take?
Most residential systems are installed and tested within 2–4 days once components are on-site. Larger commercial projects are scoped individually and scheduled around your operations.
Do you offer warranty?
Yes. Every component carries its manufacturer warranty, and our installation workmanship is separately guaranteed — so you're covered on both the hardware and the way it was fitted.
Can I power my entire house?
Yes, with the right panel, inverter and battery sizing. Our energy audit measures your actual appliance load — including heavier items like air conditioners and pumping systems — so the final system is built to carry your whole home, not just the essentials.
How many batteries do I need?
It depends on your nightly load and how many hours of backup you want without sun. During your site evaluation, we calculate this precisely so you're not paying for capacity you'll never use — or left short when it matters.
How long do batteries last?
Quality lithium batteries are built for thousands of charge cycles, typically translating to many years of daily use with proper care. We'll walk you through the expected lifespan of your specific battery type before you commit.
